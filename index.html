about me

Backend-first. Built for the long innings.

I'm Ajay, a software engineer in San Jose. Today I'm a software consultant at Applied Materials in Santa Clara, building the pipelines that move measurement data from semiconductor manufacturing equipment into cloud analytics.

Before that I finished my M.S. in Computer Software Engineering at San José State University, where I worked as a research assistant on cloud-native backend services.

I like systems that have to keep their shape under pressure: microservices that scale horizontally, event-driven pipelines that recover from failure, and AI agents that stay reliable through long conversations. Most of my work is in Python, Go, and Java, running on Kubernetes, AWS, and GCP.
